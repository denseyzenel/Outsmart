---
name: OUTSMART unpredictability
description: Product principle for content variety, Mystery Drops, and replay retention.
---

OUTSMART itself must remain difficult to reverse-engineer. Players should not learn a fixed question order, mechanic cadence, or reliable session formula. Use directed variety, conceptual deduplication, cross-mechanic surprises, and rare Mystery Drops rather than naive randomness.

**Why:** The user’s retention goal is “don’t let the user figure out OUTSMART.” Once the app’s own pattern becomes obvious, the human-versus-AI tension collapses.

**How to apply:** Vary challenge type and sequence across sessions, avoid adjacent conceptual repeats, preserve prior-exposure memory, and make surprises rare. Never change rules after an answer or fake an AI prediction; unpredictability must remain fair.

Category choices must reveal judgment, preference, risk tolerance, or behavioral patterns. Avoid factual quizzes and options with an objectively correct or obviously sensible answer.

**Why:** OUTSMART predicts player behavior; testing knowledge or offering a trivial answer undermines the human-versus-model premise.

**How to apply:** Give both options credible benefits and meaningful costs. Use ambiguity, competing values, incomplete evidence, and psychological tension so players must choose rather than solve.
