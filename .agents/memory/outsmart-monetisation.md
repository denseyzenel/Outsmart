---
name: OUTSMART monetisation principles
description: Product constraints that future game, billing, and account changes must preserve.
---

Keep the first onboarding game entirely free of payment prompts. Start the seven-day PRO trial only after onboarding completes, without requiring a card. Keep the free tier genuinely playable with full core sessions, a daily challenge, basic results, and progression; position PRO around mastery through memory, deeper analysis, stronger AI, history, modes, and unlimited play. The trial may unlock advanced tools, but only an active paid subscription unlocks the ten paid worlds. Pressure Test, Would You Rather, and Detective are the only free worlds. Category cards may show each player's own play count and best fool count as free progression, while detailed behavioral signals and the full session log remain PRO.

**Why:** The agreed product promise is “FREE = Can I beat the AI?” and “PRO = Can I master the AI?” Monetisation must not weaken the first-time experience or use deceptive urgency.

**How to apply:** Any future feature gate, pricing message, account flow, or new game mode should be checked against this promise. Trial-expiry messaging must state what locks, and billing must avoid fake scarcity, forced cards, or automatic trial conversion.
