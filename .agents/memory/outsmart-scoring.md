---
name: OUTSMART competitive scoring
description: Durable rules for USER-versus-AI points and Xavi's role in prediction outcomes.
---

The user and AI each begin a session with 0 points. The AI commits to a prediction before every answer, so every round has one winner. The winner gains 100 points and the other score remains unchanged.

**Why:** The score represents a direct player-versus-model contest, not generic points for completing rounds.

**How to apply:** A broken prediction awards the user +100; a correct AI prediction awards AI +100. Label the sides YOUR SCORE and AI SCORE. Preserve both in final results. Never include unscored rounds.

There are three separate entities: the user, the AI opponent, and Xavi. Xavi is the pixel cat commentator who celebrates after the user breaks an AI prediction. Do not show Xavi when the AI wins.

**Why:** Xavi is the player's companion, not a neutral announcer or AI supporter.

**How to apply:** Label the player side YOU or YOUR SCORE. Keep Xavi absent from AI-win states. Xavi's remarks should comment on the player's success from the outside.
